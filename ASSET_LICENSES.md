## New garage customization props (cleared)

The garage uses local 1K glTF downloads of Poly Haven's [Metal Tool Chest](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_tool_chest), [Old Tyre](https://polyhaven.com/a/old_tyre), [Tool Cart](https://polyhaven.com/a/tool_cart), [Metal Toolbox](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_toolbox), [Portable Welding Cart](https://polyhaven.com/a/portable_welding_cart), [Rusted Wheel Rim](https://polyhaven.com/a/rusted_wheel_rim_01), and [Oil Tin](https://polyhaven.com/a/oil_tin). Poly Haven publishes these models under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license), which permits commercial game use and redistribution without required attribution. The files in `public/garage/` were downloaded from Poly Haven's official asset CDN and checked against the MD5 values in its API manifest. The surrounding shelves, brake display, aero part, exhausts, service stands, signs, and lighting are original code-native geometry.

All 13 externally hosted GLB files at `https://race.teamtrader.co.za` were identified by directory index on 2026-10-19. The model archives, author names, source listing licenses, commercial rights and attribution requirements have **not yet been inspected**.
